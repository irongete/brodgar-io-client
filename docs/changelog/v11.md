# v11

**2026-09-30** · since v10 · [download](https://github.com/irongete/brodgar-io-client/releases/tag/v11)

A day and a night for the sky, and a view distance that reaches out to 512 grids without costing the frame.

## Client

### Sky and weather

- **Added** a day and night cycle to the improved sky: the sun follows a real path, higher in summer, and the moon rises and sets by its phase.
- **Added** lighting and shadows that follow whichever of the sun or the moon is up, and cel-shaded objects that darken at night.
- **Added** *Dark nights* and *Objects follow the light* switches under **Sky & weather**.
- **Fixed** dawn and dusk changing in a step, and the moon's lit side not facing the sun.
- **Fixed** a session started by day being lit as night until the server's first night.
- **Fixed** near clouds with pitted, streaked edges, and stripes of shadow on the ground under a low sun.

### Terrain and view distance

- **Changed** the view distance reaches up to 512 grids, from 64; a client with no range set starts at 8.
- **Changed** ground near enough for its tiles to show is drawn in full detail, as much as the memory given to the client holds; farther ground is crisper and lit like the ground beside it.
- **Added** a *LOD distance* slider under **Range** (25–200 %): how far each level of detail of the far ground reaches.
- **Improved** turning the camera builds nothing new, and panning leaves no holes.
- **Changed** the remembered ground follows the map: ground recorded again is drawn again.
- **Changed** the ground on screen loads first after login, and the far ground appears at once instead of after tens of seconds.
- **Fixed** switching the view distance off and on leaving the nearby ground undrawn, and far ground that could not be clicked.

### Objects and ghosts

- **Changed** ghosts, sprites and models past the live area are drawn on the remembered ground, at its recorded height.
- **Improved** thousands of ghosts no longer cost frame time, and a ghost past the live area no longer vanishes as the ground around it loads.
- **Fixed** translucent ghosts being painted over by the ground.

### Performance

- **Added** a *Frustum culling* option: the client leaves out what the camera cannot see, keeping it in the shadows.
- **Added** a *LOD distance* slider under **Objects**: repeated objects such as walls and palisades are drawn simpler as they shrink on screen, and not at all when smaller than a pixel or two.
- **Improved** repeated objects are drawn a map grid at a time and skipped off screen: a base full of palisades draws a fraction of the triangles it did.
- **Improved** memory use of the far terrain, about 80 % less: the whole range fits in 4 GB.
- **Improved** CPU use while the camera moves, and less garbage per frame with it still.

### Map

- **Fixed** importing a map again stopping halfway with *Could not import map: null*.
- **Changed** a map import only adds ground where nothing is recorded, and never replaces ground you recorded.

## Addon API

### Client

- **Changed** [`client():viewDistance()`](../addons/api/client/README.md#client) takes `1`..`512`, from `1`..`64`; the default is `8`.
- **Added** `culled` and `cullable` to [`p:render()`](../addons/api/client/profiling/counters.md#render): what frustum culling left out this frame, and what it could test.

### Virtual objects

- **Changed** a ghost, sprite or model standing past the streamed ground is [drawn on the remembered ground](../addons/api/virtual/README.md) at its recorded height, where it used to wait; a patch still waits.
